title: 👋 Welcome to my un-page
---
Hi! You've fallen right into my ~~trap~~ corner of the Internet. I made this space as an experiment in self-expression. It scratches an itch that I call my “share-surge”: the sudden, overwhelming urge to impart a recent experience to another person.

Anyone is susceptible to the share-surge. Whoever is in standing front of me. The first person listed in my group chat, alphabetized by last name. It could be one of my parents, my best friend, a professor from college. Imagine: “I saw this [[dogs|dog]] while I was out and I said ‘I love you’ a little too loudly”, or “you gotta see this hyper-specific [[my-sense-of-humor|meme]] that's only funny with background knowledge I know you don't have”. Congrats, you’re the next in line!

That’s why I created this website as an alternative to a social media page: my “un-page”.

Why an “un-page”?

1. **True expression is disorganized.** Platforms impose structure. They confine new ideas to post formats, character limitations, and linear timelines. An un-page embraces my messiness and offshoot trains of thought.
2. **Filtration at the cost of sincerity.** Anything on a major platform, in an instant, is visible to hundreds of peers after hitting “post”. Once it’s out, it’s out. So I pre-edit. I mask. I spend more time writing for an audience than actually sharing things.
3. **There is solace in disconnecting.** 

All of the content here is intentionally unorganized. Take a look around by following a trail of links.

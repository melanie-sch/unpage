Hi! I made this website to write about random things. I think of it as a departure from a social media page: my *“un-page”*.

Everything here is intentionally unorganized. Take a look around by following a trail of links, pick something from the navigation, or start with one of these:

- [[Little delights|Little delights]]

## Why an “un-page”?

1. **Writing for myself first.** After hitting “post”, what we share on social media is instantly visible for all the world to see. So I pre-edit. I mask. I spend more time writing for the eyes of the people I know than actually sharing things. This is *my* space to be authentically me.
2. **Letting my thoughts roam freely.** Major social media platforms impose structure, constraining new content to post formats, character limitations, and linear timelines. I have the most fun when I jump around between ideas and follow offshoot trains of thought.
3. **Disconnecting.** Social media feeds are full of highlight reels and doomscrolling traps. Being active on social media isn’t a bad thing, but being too absorbed is exhausting. Writing things here is a healthier, more productive outlet for me.

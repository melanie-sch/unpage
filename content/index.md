Hi! You've fallen right into my ~~trap~~ corner of the Internet. I made this space as an experiment in self-expression. It scratches an itch that I call my “share-switch”: the sudden, overwhelming urge to impart a recent experience to another person.

Anyone is susceptible to the share-switch. My best friend. A random Discord channel. A teacher I had class with years ago. Imagine: “I saw this [[dogs|dog]] while I was out and I said ‘I love you’ a little too loudly.” Or, “you gotta see this hyper-specific [[my-sense-of-humor|meme]] that's only funny with background knowledge I know you don’t have.” Congrats, you’re next in line!

That’s why I created this website as an alternative to a social media page: my “un-page”.

All of the content here is intentionally unorganized. Take a look around by following a trail of links, or pick something from the navigation.

![](https://usagif.com/wp-content/uploads/gifs/sunflower-61.gif)

## Why an “un-page”?

1. **Letting thoughts roam freely.** Platforms impose structure. They constrain new ideas to post formats, character limitations, and linear timelines. An un-page lets me embrace my messiness and offshoot trains of thought.
2. **Writing for myself first.** After hitting “post”, what we share on social media is instantly visible to hundreds of people we know. So I pre-edit. I mask. I spend more time writing for the eyes of other people than actually sharing things. An un-page is *my* space to be authentically me.
3. **Disconnecting.** Social media feeds are full of highlight reels and doomscrolling traps. Being active on social media isn’t a bad thing, but being too absorbed is exhausting. An un-page is a healthier, more productive alternative for me.

A big part of my [[my-sense-of-humor|sense of humor]]. 

I’m indulging in a pun-cracking competition with another person. We never explicitly challenged each other. We both came to an unspoken agreement that, if one person slips a pun into the conversation without the other noticing, then they just got played.

Only once have they caught me off guard. I owned up to it. Of course, it didn’t go over without a swear word or two. But they know I can dish ‘em out, too.

A big part of my [[my-sense-of-humor|sense of humor]]. Everyone in my social circle knows that, out of anyone, I have the strongest pun game. Must be from playing a lot of [[game-shows|word games]].

I don’t fully understand what it is about puns makes people groan.

## Out-playing with words

I’m indulging in a pun-cracking competition with another person. We never explicitly challenged each other. We both came to an unspoken agreement that, if one person slips a pun into the conversation without the other noticing, then they just got played.

Only once have they caught me off guard. I owned up to it. Of course, it didn’t go over without a swear word or two.

I have the aptitude. My timing is okay. Problem is, I have a serious handicap: I have no [[social-deception|poker face]].

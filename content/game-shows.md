The first game show I ever watched was called Lingo. It combined the game mechanics of bingo and Wordle, before Wordle was even a thing. To give you an idea, here’s [an older episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sC0kie6dPjo&t=80s) you can watch.

Later on, my family introduced me to Wheel of Fortune, Chain Reaction, and Jeopardy. My first roommate even subscribed to the Game Show Network just for me. When she told me, I [[little-delights|couldn’t stop smiling]] for weeks.

I don’t want to admit how many episodes of Jeopardy I have recorded on DVR.

## My favorite game shows

1. Cash Cab. (Ben Bailey is the GOAT.)
2. Jeopardy!
3. The $100,000 Pyramid.

## My *least* favorite game shows

1. Bingo Blitz.
2. Match Game.
3. Press Your Luck.
4. The Wall.

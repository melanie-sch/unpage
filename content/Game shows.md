The first game show I ever watched was called Lingo. It combined the game mechanics of bingo and Wordle, before Wordle was even a thing. To give you an idea, here’s [an older episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sC0kie6dPjo&t=80s) you can watch.

Later on, my family introduced me to Wheel of Fortune, Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?, and Jeopardy. My first roommate even subscribed to the Game Show Network just for me. When she told me, I [[Little delights|couldn’t stop smiling]] for weeks.

I don’t want to admit how many episodes of Jeopardy I have recorded on DVR. Let’s just say it’s enough to flatter Ken Jennings.

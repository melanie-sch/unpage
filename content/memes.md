[Brittanica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/meme) defines a meme as a:

> “unit of cultural information spread by imitation.”

…but us common folk, outside of academia, would say a meme is, well, anything online. You and I could be a meme. He’s a meme. She’s a meme. That video, number, song, fictional character? A meme.

“Social deception? Um, don’t you mean *lying?*”

Hah. Deceived. 

I deceived you by typing these words. I would have also deceived you if I was speaking to you in person. Where you could see how hard I’m smiling. And hear me nose exhale.

I am no liar. Because *I’m bad at lying*.

What I really mean is harmlessly misdirecting others for some good, wholesome fun. 

## Social deception tasks that are difficult for me

1. **Playing strategy games.** I am *horrendous* at playing Among Us.
2. **Keeping a surprise secret.** “Why no, dear Mother. There is no such ‘weekend vacation’ planned by your husband for your anniversary. He *certainly* never informed me of anything like that…”
